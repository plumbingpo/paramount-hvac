Keeping Your San Diego HVAC System Running Longer

Most mid-season breakdowns trace back to skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Swap your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never feel comfortable, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and much easier on you than an emergency one during the first heat wave of summer.

Minor issues don't stay minor when they're ignored. Consider a weak capacitor: replacing it is a quick, inexpensive repair, but if it's ignored for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and suddenly a minor fix has become a full system replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and call at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and prevents a few miserable nights.

Common HVAC Problems in San Diego

Most local HVAC failures announce themselves before they happen. A breaker that trips every few days, a condenser that hums louder each month, rooms drifting further from the thermostat setting, energy bills creeping up with nothing else changed. These are the early symptoms of failing capacitors, refrigerant leaks, dirty coils, and duct problems, and caught early they're a quick visit. Ignored, they take out compressors and heat exchangers, the two most expensive parts in the system. We find the root cause instead of treating symptoms, so small problems stay small.
